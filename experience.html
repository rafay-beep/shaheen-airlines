Cockpit View

Every Shaheen Airlines flight is led by a two-pilot crew working from a modern glass cockpit, with digital flight displays, weather radar, and an autopilot & flight management system that keeps every leg smooth from pushback to touchdown.

Curious passengers are welcome to peek in during boarding on select flights — ask your cabin crew if a flight deck visit is available before departure.

Onboard Lounges

Passengers can relax during the flight in our dedicated onboard lounge, featuring comfortable seating, complimentary refreshments, and a spacious atmosphere to stretch, socialize, or unwind away from their seats.

On our long-haul Boeing 777, passengers can step into the onboard social lounge — a shared space to stretch, chat, or enjoy a drink mid-flight before returning to their seats and continuing their journey in comfort.
