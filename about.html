Our Journey

1993
Company Founded

Shaheen Airlines took flight for the first time, launching domestic services from Karachi with a single aircraft and a promise to connect Pakistan's cities safely and affordably.

2005
International Flights Started

We spread our wings beyond Pakistan's borders, launching our first international routes into the Middle East and opening new opportunities for travelers and businesses alike.

2012
Fleet Expanded

Our growing fleet welcomed new Airbus and Boeing aircraft, letting us add more routes, more frequent departures, and more comfort for passengers across our network.

2020
Digital Booking System

We launched our online booking platform, giving passengers the ability to search flights, choose their seats, and book tickets from anywhere in just a few clicks.

2026
1 Million Passengers

We proudly welcomed our one millionth passenger, a milestone built on three decades of trust, safety, and dedicated service to everyone who flies with us.
